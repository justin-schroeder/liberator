The app has a fixed 640×730-point content area; image caps preserve the painted header's proportions.

## Final revision

The final interface has no aircraft images or B-24 subtitle. Its title is “Liberator”. The Dock icon contains only the enlarged aircraft, with no pinup or backing plate, and retains fifteen Apple tally marks.

The final master is a 1312×1199 RGBA PNG with genuine transparency. AppKit packages it into square icon sizes with proportional scaling to 99.5% of the canvas width. `DockIcon.png` supplies a custom, transparent NSDockTile image view while the app runs, so the aircraft is displayed without a backing tile. The 512-pixel Dock image has alpha-zero corners and aircraft bounds at x=1…506.

The scanning instrument is native Canvas artwork in `Sources/RadarView.swift`, with a broad sweep trail, etched rings, a metal bezel, and collision-aware filename callouts. It uses the original unlettered panel texture in `Resources/AgedPanelBare.png` while scanning, then restores the painted title and paper receipt for the findings list.
